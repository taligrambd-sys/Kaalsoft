Welcome to Webkaal (developed by Kaalsoft). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our application. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices for the Webkaal Android App.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Webkaal is engineered as a client-side offline-first IDE and APK compiler tool. We strictly prioritize your confidentiality:

  • No Personal Identity Data: We do not ask for, collect, or store names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • No Project Code Uploads: All HTML, CSS, JavaScript, assets, and project compilations occur locally on your device storage. We never upload your intellectual property or source code to any cloud servers.
  • No Financial Data: The application does not collect payment or banking information.

2. Local Device Storage & Permissions

To provide full IDE and compiling functionality, Webkaal utilizes standard Android device permissions:

  • Storage / File Access: Used exclusively to create project directories, save code files, import/export zip backups, and generate output APK packages.
  • Internet Access: Used only for checking app version updates, loading web documentation, or rendering web preview requests initiated by the user.

3. Third-Party Services & Ads

Webkaal may integrate standard and compliant third-party SDKs to sustain development and provide runtime analytics:

  • Google Play Services / Firebase: For basic crash analytics and update verification.
  • Ad Networks (e.g. Google AdMob): If ads are displayed, non-personally identifiable advertising IDs may be processed in compliance with Google Play Policies.

🔒 Total Source Code Ownership: Any APK generated using Webkaal belongs 100% to you. We claim zero ownership or telemetry rights over the apps you build.

4. Children's Privacy

Webkaal does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, our services comply with COPPA and global child safety standards.
